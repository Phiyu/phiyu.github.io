### Why parity

General relativity and the standard model of the photon are parity-even, so a detected preference for one handedness on cosmological scales points to new physics: a pseudoscalar field, a Chern–Simons term, or chiral dynamics during inflation.

### Where it can show up

For a scalar field the power spectrum and bispectrum are blind to parity, because two- and three-point configurations can always be rotated into their mirror images. The lowest parity-odd scalar statistic is the four-point function [@cahn2021]. Tensors and polarisation offer more direct channels. A rotation of the CMB polarisation plane by an angle $\beta$ converts E-modes into B-modes [@lue1999],

$$
C_\ell^{EB,\rm obs} = \tfrac12 \sin(4\beta)\left(C_\ell^{EE} - C_\ell^{BB}\right),
$$

and chiral gravitational waves source TB and EB correlations at low multipoles.
