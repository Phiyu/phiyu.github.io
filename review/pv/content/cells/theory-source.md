### Chern–Simons gravity

Coupling a pseudoscalar to $R\tilde R$ splits the two tensor helicities, so the primordial background is circularly polarised [@alexander2009; @alexander2006].

### Axion–gauge-field dynamics

A pseudoscalar coupled to $F\tilde F$ amplifies one helicity of the gauge field, which in turn sources chiral tensors and parity-odd scalar correlators [@sorbo2011].

### Parity-odd scalar trispectrum

The scalar sector can carry parity violation only from the four-point function onwards [@shiraishi2016].
